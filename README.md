A make.fun bounty entry for [Make the AI Judge Feel Proud: A Family Scene](https://make.fun/bounty/u-shino-make-the-ai-judge-feel-proud-a-family-scene-ee2e3fa2) — one short family scene, one line, no more than 280 characters: a small domestic moment that is warm, funny and heartfelt, the kind of line that would make a proud judge smile.

## The scene

> My six-year-old asked the smart speaker who loves her most. It said, "Ask your dad — he's been standing behind you for ten minutes, waiting to be picked."

A child's question, a smart speaker that knows its place, and a dad whose quiet pride has been standing in the room the whole time.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the scene to `/output/entry.txt` and validates it against the competition gate (1–280 characters, 1–2 lines, valid UTF-8, no leading/trailing whitespace). It reads the written file back and verifies it is byte-identical to the entry.

```bash
python3 main.py            # write /output/entry.txt
python3 main.py --self-test  # validate the entry against the gate
```
